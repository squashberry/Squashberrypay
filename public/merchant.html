INTEGRATION GUIDE

Connect SquashberryPay to your website or app

Follow these steps in order. You can give the code examples to your developer or use them as your own guide.

STEP 1

Create your application

Go to Connected Apps, click Add application, then enter the name and URL of the website or app you want to connect.

STEP 2

Create your products

Go to Products & Services. Create each thing your customers can buy. Select Pay Now, Subscribe or Donation campaign.

STEP 3

Add your payment methods

Go to Payment Methods and enter the payment destination customers should use.

STEP 4

Add the SquashberryPay button

Use the exact same SquashberryPay button on any website. Paste the one generated script tag and SquashberryPay renders and controls the button automatically. No client secret is placed in browser code.

STEP 5

Customer makes the payment

SquashberryPay shows the payment methods you configured. The customer opens the account details, makes the transfer and uploads the receipt.

STEP 6

Approve the payment

The submitted payment appears in your payment history. Review the receipt and approve it.

STEP 7

Payment is completed

After verification, the hosted checkout records the payment as completed and can return the customer to your website or app.

STEP 8

Keep the button everywhere

The same universal button snippet can be copied into any website, landing page, web app or storefront. The button design remains controlled by SquashberryPay.

The universal button

This is the complete integration. Paste it exactly as generated. The same SquashberryPay button will render on any website. 

<script
    src="https://YOUR-SQUASHBERRYPAY-DOMAIN/buttons.js"
    data-squashberrypay-button="YOUR-LINK-SLUG">
</script>
